About

Leesharam Alexander Rajcoomar

I am a 2B student in Accounting and Financial Management, Analytics stream, University of Waterloo. My prior co-op work was preparing Canadian corporate and personal tax returns, which is where I learned that the difference between knowing a rule and being able to apply it under time pressure is most of the job.

Why this site exists

Most of what I build starts as a problem I have: a course that will not stay in my head, a claim I do not believe, a process I keep repeating by hand. The output is usually an interactive document, because a diagram you can interrogate beats a paragraph you can skim. Rather than let those sit in a downloads folder, they live here, running, where anyone can use them.

How the work is organised

  • Apps are interactive: drills, trainers and daily instruments. Several install to a phone home screen and run offline.
  • Essays are data pieces where the argument is carried by the figures, and every figure carries the source it was published under.
  • References are built for retrieval under pressure rather than for reading front to back.

Interests

Financial reporting under IFRS and ASPE, Canadian tax, and the analytics side of accounting. Outside coursework I follow AI in medicine and commercial spaceflight, and I run a standing personal research program on the science of learning, judgment under uncertainty, and capital cycles.

A note on the material

These are my own study artefacts, written by me for my own use. They are not course materials, not official solutions, and not a substitute for the standards themselves. Where a figure or a rule matters, check the primary source: the CPA Canada Handbook, the Income Tax Act, or the CRA.
